The **Modern Modeling Methods (M3)** conference is an interdisciplinary conference designed
to showcase the latest modeling methods and to present research related to these
methodologies. The 11th M3 conference is preceded by an all-day pre-conference workshop on
Stan with Bob Carpenter and Mitzi Morris on Monday, June 22.

Keynote speakers for this year's conference include Jennifer Hill, Gregory Hancock, and
Patrick Curran.
