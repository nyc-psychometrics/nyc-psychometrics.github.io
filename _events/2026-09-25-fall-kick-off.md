Join our NYC Psychometrics kick-off event. Our plan is to eat some pizza, mingle with other measurement minded people, learn a bit about people's research interests and projects, and perhaps strategize a bit about what kinds of events people would be interested in attending.  Because we do have security out front and also to ensure that we order enough (but not too much!) pizza, please take a minute to fill out the survey if you plan to come on 9/25. And even if you can't attend, fill out the survey so you hear about (and can weigh in on) other upcoming events and activities.
