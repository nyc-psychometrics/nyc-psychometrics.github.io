Our [Google Group](https://groups.google.com/g/psychometrics-nyc) is the hub of the community. Joining gets you:

- Announcements about talks, happy hours, reading groups, and meetups
- A place to ask questions, share work, and find collaborators
- Connections to [doctoral programs](/programs/) across the city

### How to join

<a class="btn btn-sm z-depth-0" role="button" href="https://groups.google.com/g/psychometrics-nyc" target="_blank" style="border: 1px solid; padding: 8px 16px; border-radius: 6px;">Join the Google Group →</a>

Prefer email? Send a message to <a href="mailto:psychometrics-nyc@googlegroups.com">psychometrics-nyc@googlegroups.com</a> to get in touch.

Everyone with an interest in measurement is welcome — students, faculty, practitioners,
and the curious alike.
